Can a quantum computer learn how to compile its own dynamics? *Scalable quantum dynamics compilation via quantum machine learning* is out in [Physical Review Research](https://doi.org/10.1103/wswv-nq6d) — with Roeland Wiersema (Vector & Waterloo), [Juan Carrasquilla](https://www.phys.ethz.ch/the-department/people/person-detail.jcarrasquill.html) (ETH Zürich), Lukasz Cincio (Los Alamos) and [Yong Baek Kim](https://sites.google.com/view/ybkimgroup/yong-baek-kim) (Toronto).
