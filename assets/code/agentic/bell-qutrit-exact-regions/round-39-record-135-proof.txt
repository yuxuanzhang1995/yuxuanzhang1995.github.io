FULL FROZEN PROOF

Let omega=exp(2 pi i/3), X|j>=|j+1 mod3>, Z|j>=omega^j|j>, and |Phi>=(|00>+|11>+|22>)/sqrt3. Put |B_U>=(U tensor I)|Phi> for U=I,X,Z. Throughout, entropy is measured in bits.

1. Reduction to a scalar phase minimum. Every pure state in the support of the target is |psi_c>=c0|B_I>+c1|B_X>+c2|B_Z>, sum|ci|²=1. Put ri=|ci|² and M=(c0 I+c1 X+c2 Z)/sqrt3. Its reduced density matrix is MM†. Multiplication using ZX=omega XZ gives
Tr(MM†)=1, Tr((MM†)²)=(2-sum ri²)/3, det(MM†)=|c0³+c1³+c2³|²/27.
For the determinant identity, I and Z are diagonal and X is a three-cycle, so det(c0I+c1X+c2Z)=c0³+c1³+c2³. For the second moment, the nonzero Weyl traces in the fourth-order expansion are the equal-index and paired-index terms; their sum is [sum ri²+2 sum(i!=j) ri rj]/3=(2-sum ri²)/3. Thus the second elementary symmetric polynomial of the spectrum is e=(1+sum ri²)/6.

At fixed e and trace1, entropy is nondecreasing in the determinant D. For distinct positive eigenvalues lambda_i, implicit differentiation of z³-z²+ez-D=0 gives dlambda_i/dD=1/prod(j!=i)(lambda_i-lambda_j). The sums of these denominators' reciprocals vanish. Using the integral representation of log and partial fractions therefore gives
 dH/dD=(1/ln2) integral_0^infinity dt/prod_i(t+lambda_i)>0.
Continuity extends monotonicity through repeated or zero eigenvalues. Phase choices independently set the arguments of ci³. The polygon inequality gives the attainable minimum modulus max(0,2max ri^(3/2)-sum ri^(3/2)). Hence the minimum pure-state entropy at r is epsilon(r), the entropy of the three nonnegative roots of
 z³-z²+[(1+sum ri²)/6]z-D(r)=0,
 D(r)=[max(0,2max ri^(3/2)-sum ri^(3/2))]²/27.
This also proves that epsilon is symmetric in the three probabilities. No convex-roof equality follows from phase minimization alone.
