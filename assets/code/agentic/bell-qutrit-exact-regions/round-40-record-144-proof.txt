FULL FROZEN PROOF

Write u=(x,y), r(u)=(x,(1-x+y)/2,(1-x-y)/2), u0=(1/2,0), and f(u)=epsilon(r(u)). We first establish the phase formula and explain the independently reproducible numerical certificate, then prove that the moving tangents are global supports.

1. Phase minimum. A normalized vector in the three-Bell support has coefficients c_i with |c_i|^2=r_i. Its reduced spectrum is the squared singular-value spectrum of A=(c0 I+c1 X+c2 Z)/sqrt(3); using the opposite tensor-factor convention merely transposes the coefficient matrix and leaves these singular values unchanged. Direct multiplication using ZX=omega XZ gives Tr(AA†)=1, Tr((AA†)^2)=(2-sum r_i^2)/3, and det(AA†)=|c0^3+c1^3+c2^3|^2/27. Consequently its characteristic polynomial is z^3-z^2+e(r)z-D.

For fixed trace and e, entropy is increasing with D on the physical spectral interval. At distinct positive roots, implicit differentiation and the integral representation of logarithm give dH/dD=(1/ln 2) integral_0^infinity [product_i(t+lambda_i)]^(-1) dt>0. Continuity extends the ordering to endpoints with repeated or zero roots. Independent choices of the arguments of c_i^3 and the polygon inequality therefore give D_min=[max(0,2 max_i r_i^(3/2)-sum_i r_i^(3/2))]^2/27. Thus epsilon is symmetric in r and equals the entropy of the cubic roots with this determinant. On the region in the claim the maximizing coordinate is r0, the expression inside the positive part is strictly positive, and the stated phases attain its minimum.

3. Quantitative moving tangents. Let |u_x-1/2|<eta, |u_y|<eta, and first suppose u_y>=0. Such u belongs to the interior of K and to W. Define T_u(v)=f(u)+grad f(u) dot (v-u), and T_0(v)=E+m(v_x-1/2). Inside K, the segment from u to v stays in K; positive-definite Hessian and Taylor's integral remainder give f(v)>=T_u(v). We have proved global support on W.

This is not yet sufficient without handling unequal tangent coefficients. Write g=grad f(u). Evenness gives f_y(u_x,0)=0, and Hyy>0 on K gives g_y>=0 when u_y>=0. In probability coordinates, T_u has coefficients (g_x,g_y,-g_y), in descending order. For any probability vector s, let s^downarrow have descending entries. All tangents in the asserted open two-dimensional region are therefore global supports.

4. Convex roof and optimal ensemble. Every pure state in a decomposition of rho(r) belongs to its three-dimensional support: the average squared norm orthogonal to that support is zero. This lower bound concerns all decompositions, not merely decompositions on a symmetric slice.

The phase-minimizing vector with coefficients (sqrt(r0),exp(i*pi/3)sqrt(r1),exp(i*pi/3)sqrt(r2)) has entropy epsilon(r). Local unitaries preserve entropy, so this ensemble attains the lower bound. This proves the claimed formula and optimality.
