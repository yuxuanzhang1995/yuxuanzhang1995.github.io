How do you tell which *mixed-state* phase a noisy quantum computer is actually in? With [Timothy Hsieh](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/people/timothy-hsieh) and [Yijian Zou](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/people/yijian-zou) at Perimeter and [Yong Baek Kim](https://sites.google.com/view/ybkimgroup/yong-baek-kim) at Toronto, we answered it on Quantinuum's H1 with Rényi correlators and a variational decoder — now out in [Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73814-6).
