*Heuristic Quantum Advantage with Peaked Circuits* was named **\#1 Best Paper** out of the 56 accepted in the Quantum Algorithms track at [IEEE Quantum Week (QCE) 2026](https://qce.quantum.ieee.org/2026/). Our largest instance — all-to-all connectivity, 2000 two-qubit gates — returns the target bitstring on Quantinuum's System Model H2 in **under two hours**; extrapolating the strongest classical simulators we could bring to bear (tensor networks with belief propagation, Pauli-path) puts the same instance at **years on Frontier and Summit**. And because a peaked circuit has a checkable answer, this is advantage you can *verify*, not merely assert. Joint work with Hrant Gharibyan and the rest of the team at [BlueQubit](https://www.bluequbit.io/) — the circuits are [public](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25838), and we invite the community to try to break them.
