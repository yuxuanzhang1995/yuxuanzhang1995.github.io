It is not just how much magic a circuit has, but where you put it: pile every T gate into a single layer and the circuit becomes classically simulable. *Classical Simulability of Quantum Circuits with Shallow Magic Depth*, with Yifan Zhang (Princeton), appeared in [PRX Quantum](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.010337) and was selected for the journal's **International Year of Quantum (IYQ) Collection**.
