We watched a **supersonic mode** travel across an 18-site fermionic chain on Quantinuum's H1 — a correlation front that the Lieb–Robinson bound forbids in any Hermitian system. *Observation of a non-Hermitian supersonic mode on a trapped-ion quantum computer*, in [Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57930-3) with [Juan Carrasquilla](https://www.phys.ethz.ch/the-department/people/person-detail.jcarrasquill.html) and [Yong Baek Kim](https://sites.google.com/view/ybkimgroup/yong-baek-kim).
