Two preprints on peaked circuits — to my mind the rare quantum-advantage proposal that is near-term feasible, classically hard, *and* verifiable all at once: *Complexity and hardness of random peaked circuits* ([arXiv:2510.00132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00132), single-author), and *Heuristic Quantum Advantage with Peaked Circuits* ([arXiv:2510.25838](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25838)) with the team at [BlueQubit](https://www.bluequbit.io/), which also shows that deciding whether a circuit is peaked is QCMA-complete.
