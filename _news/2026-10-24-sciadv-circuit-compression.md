*Circuit compression for 2D quantum dynamics* is accepted at **Science Advances**. Using Pauli propagation we compress the dynamics of lattices up to **30 × 30 qubits**, beating standard Trotterization by orders of magnitude at equal circuit depth — and on Quantinuum's H1 the compiled ansatz tracks the dynamics far more faithfully than the Trotterized circuit. Led by Matteo D'Anna, with Roeland Wiersema, Manuel Rudolph and [Juan Carrasquilla](https://www.phys.ethz.ch/the-department/people/person-detail.jcarrasquill.html).
