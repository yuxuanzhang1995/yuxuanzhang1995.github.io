*Evaluating Large Language Models in Scientific Discovery* ([arXiv:2512.15567](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15567)) — a large multi-institution effort led by Zhangde Song, asking whether LLMs can actually run the loop of hypothesis, observation, and revision, rather than recite decontextualized facts.
